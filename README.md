Nahian: The Ganja Seeker
An ancient tale of betrayal, forbidden magic, and a desperate escape.

Long, long ago, in an age when magic ruled the earth and the dead whispered beneath its surface, there lived a mysterious wanderer named Nahian, the Ganja Seeker.

For decades, Nahian had served Mihal, the Guardian of the Underworld — an ancient and terrifying being who commanded the spirits of the dead. Through years of loyalty, obedience, and unquestioning service, Nahian slowly earned Mihal's trust.

But beneath that mask of devotion, Nahian had a secret ambition.

He sought the legendary Wand of Eternity, a powerful magical relic capable of bending the laws of nature and commanding forces beyond human understanding. The wand belonged to Mihal, who guarded it deep within his forbidden realm.

One fateful night, when the shadows were at their darkest, Nahian finally made his move.

He stole the wand.

Without looking back, he fled through the ancient corridors of the Underworld, carrying the most powerful artifact ever created.

But his freedom would not come easily.

The moment Mihal discovered the betrayal, his fury shook the foundations of the Underworld. With a thunderous roar, he unleashed his army of restless spirits, cursed souls, and terrifying creatures to hunt down the traitor.

Now, Nahian must survive a deadly journey through haunted lands, forgotten ruins, and the twisted pathways of the Underworld.

Every shadow could hide a spirit. Every corridor could become his grave.

And Mihal will stop at nothing to reclaim his wand.

This is the story of Nahian — the Ganja Seeker.

He spent decades earning a demon's trust. It took only one night to become his greatest enemy.
